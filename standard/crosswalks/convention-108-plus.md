# Crosswalk: Convention 108+

**Baseline:** Council of Europe, consolidated text of the modernised *Convention for the Protection of Individuals with regard to Automatic Processing of Personal Data* (Convention 108, ETS No. 108), as amended by the *Protocol amending the Convention* opened in Strasbourg on 2018-10-10 (CETS No. 223), commonly called Convention 108+ (`SRC-0004`).
**Last reviewed:** 2026-08-14

<!-- oasps-citations:start -->
The official Council of Europe materials provide a data-protection and human-rights anchor for lawful and fair processing, proportionality, data minimization, transparency, controller accountability, safeguards for data flows, and independent supervision. This is a principle-level interpretation of the consolidated modernised text, not a legal opinion about applicability or treaty compliance. [SRC-0004]
<!-- oasps-citations:end -->

| Convention 108+ principle area | Related OASPS requirements | OASPS surveillance-specific extension |
|---|---|---|
| Lawfulness, fairness, and proportionality | OASPS-B02–B04, OASPS-D01–D03, OASPS-F01 | Require a structured investigative predicate and visible democratic authorization for consequential public surveillance. |
| Transparency and intelligible information | OASPS-A01–A04, OASPS-C06, OASPS-E02, OASPS-E05 | Disclose derived attributes, correlations, capability semantics, local configuration, and rights-relevant documentation history. |
| Purpose limitation and data quality | OASPS-B02–B05, OASPS-D05–D06 | Make search purpose reviewable, restrict protected-activity use, expose sharing, and align enforceable contracts with public secondary-use statements. |
| Data minimization and storage limitation | OASPS-D01–D04 | Separate targeted evidence preservation from ordinary population-scale retention and verify deletion across backups, replicas, and indexes. |
| Controller accountability and privacy by design | OASPS-C01–C05, OASPS-E01–E06 | Require public inspectability by default, complete rights-relevant auditability, and evidence linking reviewed safeguards to production. |
| Independent and effective supervision | OASPS-C05, OASPS-F02–F05 | Give oversight bodies meaningful evidence, authority, suspension paths, and remedies rather than agency-selected excerpts alone. |
| Data-flow safeguards | OASPS-B05, OASPS-C02, OASPS-D05 | Make cross-agency and secondary-use flows visible, controlled, logged, and jurisdiction-aware. |

## Limits

Convention principles support the direction of many OASPS requirements, including independent supervision, but they do not automatically establish OASPS's specific technical mechanisms or E01's restricted-review approval test. Public inspectability, reproducible builds, deployment attestation, and the exact design of attempted-misuse logs are OASPS proposals requiring further expert review.

This crosswalk does not determine whether a particular country, agency, vendor, or deployment is legally bound by or compliant with Convention 108+.
