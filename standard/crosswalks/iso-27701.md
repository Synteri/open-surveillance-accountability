# Crosswalk: ISO/IEC 27701:2025

**Baseline:** ISO/IEC 27701:2025, Edition 2, *Information security, cybersecurity and privacy protection — Privacy information management systems — Requirements and guidance*, using only ISO's official public overview (`SRC-0007`).
**Last reviewed:** 2026-08-14

<!-- oasps-citations:start -->
ISO's public overview describes ISO/IEC 27701:2025 as a Privacy Information Management System standard for PII controllers and processors and identifies it as Edition 2, published in October 2025. OASPS uses only that high-level public scope as an anchor for privacy governance, risk management, accountability, demonstrable controls, and continual improvement. [SRC-0007]
<!-- oasps-citations:end -->

## High-level mapping only

| Privacy-management area | Related OASPS requirements | OASPS surveillance-specific extension |
|---|---|---|
| Processing inventory and accountability | OASPS-A01–A04, OASPS-D05–D06 | Publish surveillance-derived attributes and make enforceable secondary-use rights match public explanations. |
| Access, purpose, and organizational controls | OASPS-B01–B05 | Attach reviewable investigative purpose, case linkage, protected-activity restrictions, and visible cross-agency sharing. |
| Monitoring, audit, and evidence of control operation | OASPS-C01–C06 | Expose complete rights-relevant events and make evidence available to independent public-interest oversight. |
| Data minimization, retention, and deletion | OASPS-D01–D04 | Separate evidence preservation from broad retention and account for backups, replicas, derived indexes, and legal holds. |
| Supplier and implementation assurance | OASPS-E01–E06, OASPS-F03 | Default rights-relevant components to public inspectability, govern any temporary restricted review independently, and link reviewed artifacts to deployed versions. |
| Governance and continual improvement | OASPS-E05, OASPS-F01–F05 | Add democratic authorization, transparent criticism, suspension triggers, and remedies for public surveillance. |

## Licensing and conformity limits

This repository does not reproduce ISO text and does not make clause-level mappings. The public overview does not establish OASPS-E01's specific disclosure or restricted-review conditions. A complete mapping requires lawful access to the current standard and qualified privacy-management review.

Nothing here establishes ISO certification, ISO conformity, or ISO endorsement of OASPS. OASPS case-study assessments are not ISO audits.
