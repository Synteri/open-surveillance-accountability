# Connecticut ALPR Accountability Pilot: funding and commissioning package

**Start here.** This folder is the minimum package needed to ask for funding or a paid commission now. The default sale is the **$5,000 Full Independent Assessment**, with Milford, Connecticut as the proposed first case.

OASPS remains a vendor-neutral public working draft. The proposed engagement is an independent, evidence-grounded review, not advocacy for or against a vendor, a certification, or legal advice.

## What to send

For a prospective payer or founding underwriter, send:

1. [One-page pilot brief](pilot-brief.md), or the [print-ready HTML version](pilot-brief.html)
2. [Offers and pricing](offers.md)
3. [Illustrative Milford preview](milford-preview.md)

For a validator or introducer, send the pilot brief and Milford preview. Ask for a brief methodology reaction and one specific funding introduction.

For a government or policy contact, send the pilot brief. Ask what evidence they currently lack and whether a research, procurement, grant, or partnership route exists if the capability is useful.

## Default commercial path

1. Choose five priority recipients from the tracker.
2. Personalize only the bracketed opening line in the payer message.
3. Attach or link the pilot brief, offers, and Milford preview.
4. Ask directly whether the recipient will fund or commission the $5,000 assessment.
5. If the answer is no, ask for one named introduction.
6. Use the call script when interest appears.
7. After a written yes, populate the agreement and invoice. Do not begin the full paid assessment before the $2,500 kickoff payment is received.

## Before the first send

These are human choices, not additional writing tasks:

- Confirm the sending email address and signature block.
- Decide whether the recipient should receive a repository link, attachments, or both.
- Review every `NEEDS_VERIFICATION` item in the target tracker before naming a person or relying on a funding route.
- Do not imply that ACLU of Connecticut, Have I Been Flocked, Alternative Newsweekly Foundation, Milford, or any other organization endorses the project.
- Do not promise access to nonpublic records, a legal conclusion, a particular finding, or a procurement outcome.
