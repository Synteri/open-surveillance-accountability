# Internal fiscal-sponsorship brief

**Purpose:** Open grant and tax-deductible-giving routes that require an eligible nonprofit recipient, without treating sponsorship as funding or changing the commercial identity of OVS Intelligence LLC.

This is an operating note, not tax or legal advice. Terms were checked against public pages on August 16, 2026 and require direct confirmation before relying on them.

## Why sponsorship may help

Some foundations and donor-advised funds can pay only an eligible 501(c)(3) recipient. A fiscal sponsor may receive restricted charitable funds, provide the sponsor's nonprofit documentation, oversee compliance, and disburse approved project expenses. Sponsorship does not itself provide money, guarantee a grant, turn the LLC into a nonprofit, or determine that Jacob or OVS may be paid.

## Best current lead: Alternative Newsweekly Foundation

[Alternative Newsweekly Foundation](https://altnewsfoundation.org/fiscal-sponsorship/) says it sponsors journalism projects, investigative and enterprise reporting, and news or information infrastructure. Its published Model C description keeps the project as a separate legal entity while ANF receives grants or donations and oversees grant compliance. The published Model C fee is 7 percent, plus transaction, banking, and possible other expenses. ANF's application includes for-profit LLC as a business-structure option, but that does not guarantee eligibility or approval.

ANF and [Have I Been Flocked](https://haveibeenflocked.com/about/funding) both publicly identify Have I Been Flocked as an ANF-sponsored project. That is a relevant precedent and a potential warm-introduction path. It is not an endorsement of OASPS, and the public pages do not identify Have I Been Flocked's sponsorship model.

## Questions ANF must answer

Mark every item below `NEEDS_VERIFICATION` until ANF answers it in writing:

- Is Model C the correct structure for the approved public-interest research and reporting activity of a project operated alongside an existing LLC?
- Can the sponsored activity coexist with separately commissioned commercial work, and what accounting and branding separation is required?
- May the approved project budget compensate Jacob or OVS for documented research and development work, and through what mechanism?
- Is the published 7 percent Model C fee current and complete, and what transaction, banking, or other charges apply?
- What is the normal application, board-review, contracting, and onboarding timeline?
- Can ANF receive private-foundation grants, donor-advised-fund grants, individual donations, corporate support, government grants, municipal funds, or fee-for-service revenue for this project?
- Who signs grant applications and reports, and what review lead time is required?
- Are expenses advanced or reimbursed, how often are funds disbursed, and is a reserve required?
- What insurance, IP, open-source licensing, public-branding, editorial-independence, termination, and unspent-fund terms apply?

## How sponsorship differs from direct commercial work

| Direct commissioned work through OVS | Sponsored public-interest work |
|---|---|
| Client buys a defined assessment under a services agreement | Donor or grantmaker supports an approved charitable project through the sponsor |
| OVS invoices and receives payment directly | Sponsor receives restricted funds and disburses approved expenses under its agreement |
| Commercial scope, tax, insurance, and reporting remain with OVS | Sponsor adds eligibility, oversight, fees, restrictions, and grant reporting |
| Best immediate route for the $2,500 or $5,000 offers | Best route for grants, donations, or a broader public pilot when the funder requires a nonprofit recipient |

The two routes can be pursued in parallel if the activities, funds, agreements, outputs, and representations are kept clear and the sponsor approves the arrangement. Do not charge both a client and a sponsored budget for the same work.

## Backups if ANF is not a fit

- [Social Good Fund](https://www.socialgoodfund.org/fiscal-sponsorship/faq/) is a faster general Model C fallback. Its public terms say an existing LLC may be considered, but fit, government-funding limits, and compensation still require review.
- [Realize Impact](https://realizeimpact.org/fiscal-sponsorship/) is a lower-administration Model C route for a mission-driven for-profit when a donor or foundation is already ready. It is not a full project administrator.
- [Code for Science & Society](https://www.codeforsociety.org/fsp/faq) is better suited to a later, larger public-interest technology program than the immediate $5,000 sale.

## Decision rule

Pursue direct commissioned work now. Pursue ANF sponsorship at the same time for grant and donation eligibility. Do not delay a willing commercial buyer while sponsorship is under review, and do not represent sponsored eligibility, tax deductibility, or compensation as approved until the sponsor confirms it.
