## Internal close checklist

Complete in this order:

1. **Confirm scope.** Name the deployment, offer, deliverables, schedule, publication assumptions, client contact, and exclusions.
2. **Get written yes.** Save the email or other written authorization that clearly accepts price and scope.
3. **Populate agreement.** Replace every bracketed field, confirm the contracting entity, and remove template instructions.
4. **Send agreement.** Request signature from the authorized client representative and countersign it.
5. **Send invoice immediately.** Use the real accounting sequence and the client's accounts-payable requirements.
6. **Collect upfront payment.** Verify cleared funds. A promise, purchase-order request, or payment screenshot is not cleared payment.
7. **Only then begin the full paid assessment.** Record the kickoff date and calculate the target delivery date.
8. **Update outreach tracker.** Record the funded status, amount, next milestone, and follow-up date without adding private payment data.

## Final pre-kickoff readback

- Contracting parties match the invoice.
- The invoice total and payment split match the selected offer.
- No work outside scope was promised in email or on the call.
- Publication and confidentiality assumptions are understood by both parties.
- Client has not been promised a particular finding, legal conclusion, certification, or procurement result.
- No raw plate data, travel history, credentials, or nonpublic security information is expected without a separately reviewed lawful need.
