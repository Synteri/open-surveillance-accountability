# First-wave funding and introduction targets

**Prepared:** 2026-08-16

**Status:** no one listed here has been contacted by this repository work
**Priority scale:** `5` means contact first; `1` means lower first-wave priority

The ranking favors a fast payment, a credible funding introduction, or removal of a structural blocker. It is an outreach order, not an assessment of organizational prestige or endorsement. Recheck every role and contact path immediately before sending. The editable operating record is [targets.csv](targets.csv).

## Recommended first five sends

| Order | Target | Primary outcome sought | Message to use |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | ACLU of Connecticut | $5,000 commission or underwriting decision; otherwise two named introductions | [Potential payer](outreach-messages.md#a-potential-payer-or-founding-underwriter) |
| 2 | Have I Been Flocked | Method critique, candid ANF experience, and a warm introduction | [Introducer or validator](outreach-messages.md#b-introducer-or-validator) |
| 3 | Alternative Newsweekly Foundation | Rapid Model C fit and onboarding decision | [Fiscal-sponsorship inquiry](outreach-messages.md#d-alternative-newsweekly-foundation-fiscal-sponsorship-inquiry) |
| 4 | Connecticut Public, The Accountability Project | Editorial interest, a publication path if warranted, and funder introductions | [Introducer or validator](outreach-messages.md#b-introducer-or-validator) |
| 5 | City of Milford | A commissioning decision or the exact procurement, evidence-access, and budget route | [Government or policy contact](outreach-messages.md#c-government-or-policy-contact) |

The first five are designed to run in parallel: a plausible underwriter, the closest specialist validator, a fiscal-sponsorship enabler, a publication partner, and the direct local commissioning route. ANF is not a payer, Have I Been Flocked is not being treated as a payer, and City outreach begins with usefulness and routing rather than a grant demand.

## Operating rule

After every send or call, update the `status`, `follow_up_date`, and `notes` fields in [targets.csv](targets.csv). Record facts, named next steps, and deadlines. Do not record assumptions as commitments, and do not treat a favorable conversation as funding until an agreement and upfront payment are complete.
