## 2. Deliverables

Researcher will deliver:

1. a criterion-by-criterion application of the current 32-criterion framework;
2. an evidence register and machine-readable evidence output;
3. a Connecticut statute, POST policy, local policy, and contract crosswalk relevant to the deployment;
4. analysis of vendor and agency claims against independently verifiable evidence;
5. analysis of data access, sharing, retention, deletion, auditability, abuse prevention, authorization, oversight, and remedy;
6. unresolved evidence questions and a prioritized set of targeted public-records request drafts;
7. recommendations; and
8. a plain-language public summary.

Client receives one factual-correction round. Corrections must identify the disputed statement and supporting evidence. Client review does not give Client control over findings, assessments, recommendations, or the decision to preserve an issue as unknown.

## 3. Independence

Researcher will follow the evidence wherever it leads and will preserve credible positive, negative, conflicting, and incomplete evidence. Client is paying for the work, not for a particular result. Researcher makes no guarantee that the assessment will support or oppose the deployment, agency, vendor, policy, or Client's position.
