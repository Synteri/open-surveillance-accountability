# Connecticut ALPR accountability offers

All three offers apply the same vendor-neutral evidence discipline: documented fact, vendor or agency assertion, inference, and unknown are kept separate. Missing public evidence is never treated as proof that a safeguard is absent.

## $2,500: Rapid Review

**Purpose:** Give a decision-maker a fast, credible view of one ALPR deployment without commissioning the complete OASPS assessment.

**Best for:** A newsroom, civil-liberties organization, municipal body, attorney, donor, or community group facing an immediate decision or meeting.

**Included:**

- One named municipal or agency deployment
- Six to ten high-priority OASPS criteria selected at kickoff
- Evidence register with links and access dates
- Clear separation of documented policy, claimed controls, deployed configuration, and independent verification
- Short statutory and policy crosswalk limited to the selected criteria
- Unresolved evidence questions and a prioritized records list
- Concise public-facing findings memo
- One factual-correction round

**Turnaround assumption:** Seven business days after kickoff payment, scope confirmation, and receipt of any client-provided records. Delayed records become documented unknowns rather than delaying delivery unless both parties agree otherwise.

**Payment:** $1,500 at kickoff and $1,000 on delivery.

**Boundaries:** No full 32-criterion application, legal opinion, certification, live-system access, security testing, exhaustive records campaign, travel, litigation support, or guarantee of a favorable or unfavorable finding.

## $5,000: Full Independent Assessment

**Default offer.** This is the engagement to lead with.

**Purpose:** Produce a complete, decision-ready assessment of one ALPR deployment that can be checked, updated, and published.

**Included:**

- Full application of the current 32-criterion OASPS framework to one named deployment
- Criterion-by-criterion evidence and assessment ledger
- Connecticut statutory, POST policy, local policy, and contract crosswalk
- Vendor claims compared with independently verifiable public evidence
- Data collection, access, sharing, retention, preservation, and deletion analysis
- Auditability, misuse prevention, authorization, oversight, and remedy analysis
- Explicit unresolved evidence questions and evidence needed to answer them
- Drafts for a targeted, privacy-preserving public-records request set
- Prioritized recommendations for the agency, public body, vendor, and independent reviewers
- Machine-readable evidence output and source register
- Public-facing plain-language summary
- One client factual-correction round that cannot control the findings

**Turnaround assumption:** Fifteen business days after kickoff payment, scope confirmation, and receipt of available baseline records. Public-record response delays do not prevent delivery. Unanswered requests remain visible as unresolved questions.

**Payment:** $2,500 at kickoff and $2,500 on delivery.

**Boundaries:** One deployment only. No legal opinion, certification, procurement guarantee, lobbying representation, lawsuit support, unauthorized testing, access to raw plate or travel data, travel, or promise of any particular conclusion. Records-request submission, fees, appeals, and extended follow-up require separate written authorization and scope.

## $25,000: Founding Connecticut Pilot

**Purpose:** Test the framework across multiple Connecticut deployments while building the public infrastructure needed for repeatable comparison and updates.

**Best for:** A foundation, major donor, statewide civil-liberties organization, research institution, newsroom collaboration, or public-interest technology funder seeking durable statewide capacity.

**Included:**

- Five to ten Connecticut ALPR deployments selected for geographic, governance, and vendor diversity
- Full or appropriately scoped deployment assessments agreed at kickoff
- Comparative scorecards that preserve evidence quality and unknowns
- Reusable evidence-ingestion and provenance workflow
- Shared statutory, POST, policy, contract, and records crosswalks
- Recurring update cycle during the pilot term
- Municipal guidance for procurement, policy, reporting, audit, and independent review
- Independent or expert review budget and documented disposition of feedback
- Machine-readable and openly licensed outputs compatible with the existing project
- Public summaries and a statewide synthesis
- Expansion beyond one vendor where public evidence supports inclusion

**Turnaround assumption:** Ten to twelve weeks from kickoff for a five-deployment pilot. A ten-deployment scope, major public-record delays, or formal outside review may require a mutually agreed schedule adjustment.

**Suggested payment:** $10,000 at kickoff, $7,500 after the midpoint evidence review, and $7,500 on final delivery.

**Boundaries:** No statewide census of every surveillance system, accreditation, legal or procurement guarantee, custom software platform, continuous monitoring after the pilot term, litigation support, raw plate data, unauthorized testing, travel unless budgeted, or guaranteed participation by agencies, vendors, journalists, or reviewers.

## Common commissioning terms

- Findings remain independent of the payer's preferred outcome.
- Source access, dates, contradictions, and unknowns remain visible.
- Public and machine-readable publication is the default, subject to lawful redaction and agreed handling of client-provided confidential material.
- Existing OASPS content remains under its published content and code licenses. Engagement-specific treatment is confirmed in the written agreement.
- Scope changes require written agreement before additional work begins.
