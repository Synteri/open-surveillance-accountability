# Unresolved Evidence Queue

These are current evidence targets, not negative findings. Any next action must be lawful, proportionate, privacy-preserving, and separately authorized when it involves contact, records requests, restricted material, or technical testing.

## Platform and vendor evidence

### Complete data and derivation inventory

- Which raw fields, metadata, vehicle descriptors, confidence values, derived attributes, indexes, feature vectors, and inference outputs exist?
- Which fields are collected, derived, searchable, displayed, exported, shared, retained, or used only internally?
- What versions and customer configurations expose each field?

**Next evidence:** a canonical versioned schema, data-flow documentation, and lawful independent implementation review.

### Vendor-privileged access

- Which vendor personnel and services can access customer data or configuration?
- Are support, security, administrative, and emergency accesses individually attributable?
- Can customers export those events, and are they available to an independent rights/use auditor?

**Next evidence:** privileged-access schema, sample redacted events, retention rule, customer export, and independent completeness test.

### Complete use and control-plane audit coverage

- What exact events are created for searches, exports, hot lists, alerts, APIs, integrations, sharing, roles, retention, filters, feature enablement, case-linked evidence preservation, legal holds, and model ingestion?
- Can an administrator or vendor suppress or alter any event?
- Does a blocked prohibited action create a privacy-preserving event?

**Next evidence:** canonical event schema, before-and-after configuration records, integrity design, and authorized completeness testing.

### Deletion and recovery paths

- What happens after nominal deletion in backups, replicas, caches, derived indexes, exports, separately preserved evidence, and legal holds?
- How are deletion exceptions authorized, recorded, expired, and tested?

**Next evidence:** storage map, lifecycle and hold rules, recovery tests, underlying assessment scope, and redacted results.

### Secondary use and machine-learning ingestion

- Which customer-derived data enters training, tuning, evaluation, or product-improvement processes?
- How do enforceable contracts limit those uses?
- What ingestion, de-identification, access, retention, and deletion evidence exists?

**Next evidence:** contract-to-policy comparison, ingestion inventory, logs, de-identification evaluation, and independent review.

### Build and production identity

- What source, dependency, model, and configuration versions were reviewed?
- Can a reviewer reproduce or otherwise identify the assessed artifact?
- What evidence links that artifact to production for a jurisdiction?

**Next evidence:** signed source and artifact identifiers, build procedure, deployment attestation, change history, and independent spot checks.

### Independent testing scope

- What did Bishop Fox test, what findings were confirmed, what was remediated, and what was retested?
- Did the review cover rights-relevant safeguards or conventional security only?
