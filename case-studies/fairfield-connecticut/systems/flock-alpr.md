## What it is

The 2024 Fairfield municipal record concerns a Flock Safety package that included automated license-plate-recognition cameras and related platform services. The product mix in the meeting packet was broader than ALPR, but the existing vendor case study evaluates only the ALPR-relevant subset. [SRC-0033]

## Operator / vendor

The records identify Flock Safety as vendor and Fairfield Police Department as the agency associated with the proposed local package. They do not independently establish execution of the displayed order form or the current 2026 production relationship. [SRC-0032, SRC-0033]

## Public purpose

The municipal approval materials present the system for police vehicle-related investigations and alerts; the current purposes actually configured or used are not established by the registered public record. [SRC-0032, SRC-0033]

## What public evidence establishes

Fairfield's Board of Selectmen unanimously approved an agreement in June 2024. An order form included in the board-approved meeting packet presents a product mix, a $110,000 annual amount, term and renewal language, and a 30-day retention term for approval. [SRC-0032, SRC-0033]

## What public evidence does not establish

The registered evidence does not independently establish execution of the displayed order form, current products or configuration, current retention and preservation settings, sharing relationships, search activity, audit access, transparency-portal availability, or present operation. [SRC-0032, SRC-0033, SRC-0036]

## Governance / authorization

The Board minutes establish public authorization of an agreement. The packet does not establish a current local ALPR policy, complete executed terms, training rules, search-purpose controls, or present oversight process. [SRC-0032, SRC-0033]

## Data and retention

The meeting-packet order form presents a 30-day retention period, but the public record does not establish that the displayed form was executed or that 30 days is the configured 2026 setting. Current deletion, preservation, export, and secondary-use behavior remain unknown. [SRC-0033]

## Access / sharing

The proposed package included sharing-capable Flock services. The current inbound and outbound partner configuration, individual searches, vendor access, and audit visibility are not established. [SRC-0033]

## Current-status confidence

The evidence is `Partially verifiable` for the documented 2024 approval and packet contents, while current implementation is `Unknown`. The absence of current proof is an evidence gap, not proof that the system is inactive or misused. [SRC-0032, SRC-0033]
