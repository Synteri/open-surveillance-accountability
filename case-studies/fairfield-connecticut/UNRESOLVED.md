## Lawful next-evidence rule

Prefer newly published official agreements, policies, plans, inventories, budgets, annual reports, transparency pages, and aggregate audit records. A human may separately decide whether to request a public record after considering privacy and requester-identification consequences; no agent should submit a request or contact an official, school, vendor, or journalist under this queue.
